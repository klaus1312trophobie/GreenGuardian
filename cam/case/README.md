# Tent camera case (PETG, Bambu A1 mini)

For an ESP32-CAM plugged onto the ESP32-CAM-MB USB board, camera module on a long flex
cable, mounted on a 19 mm tent pole. Generated by `build_case.py` (all dimensions are
parameters at the top; needs `pip install manifold3d numpy`).

| File | Part | Print |
|------|------|-------|
| `case_body.stl` | box, cavity 42 × 28.4 × 40 mm; the lower 12 mm are 3 mm wider on each long side so the two side buttons of the ESP32-CAM-MB (IO0 / RST) stay free; 20 × 23 mm opening in both short walls for the USB plug and the SD card; hinge tongue | as oriented, no supports |
| `case_lid.stl` | snap-on lid with tapered seat for the lens ring | as oriented (outside face down), no supports |
| `lens_ring_10mm.stl` … `lens_ring_14mm.stl` | lens collets; ring N grips lens barrels from N − 0.2 to N + 0.8 mm | as oriented, no supports |
| `lens_rings_10-14mm_plate.stl` | all five rings, 10 mm on the left … 14 mm on the right | – |
| `pole_clip_19mm.stl` | snap clip (18.5 mm bore, clamps on a 19 mm pole) with hinge fork | as oriented, no supports |

All files are already in print orientation. 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 15 % infill is enough.

Also needed: one M3 × 16 (or × 20) screw and one M3 nut for the hinge.

Assembly

1. Measure the lens barrel and print the matching ring (if in doubt print two neighbours).
2. Push the ring into the lid from the inside until it wedges, fingers pointing into the case.
3. Push the camera lens into the ring from the inside until its front is flush with the ring.
4. Put the board stack into the body, USB socket at one of the two openings, fold the flex
   cable loosely (no sharp kinks) and snap the lid on.
5. Nut into the hexagon of the clip, tongue between the ears, screw through.
6. Snap the clip onto the pole. Turning the clip around the pole pans the camera, the hinge
   tilts it from horizontal down to straight down. Clip upside down = horizontal to straight up.

Picture upside down or mirrored: set `CAM_VFLIP` / `CAM_HMIRROR` in `include/config.h`.
